Weekly Total is Sunday through Saturday. Green = exactly 40 hours, yellow = under 40, red = over 40. Admins can change each clinic's daily Shift Capacity from 0–4. A 12-hour shift also has a daily work-site selector. It defaults to the employee's primary clinic but can be changed for floating/cross-coverage. Regular employees cannot add a 12-hour shift when that clinic is full; admins can override after confirmation.
